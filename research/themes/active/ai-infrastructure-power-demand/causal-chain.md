# Causal Chain

```text
AI model demand + inference growth
  -> data center compute buildout
  -> higher electricity demand and denser power loads
  -> grid interconnection, generation, cooling, and equipment constraints
  -> capex and order growth in specific value-chain layers
  -> possible beneficiaries, victims, and bottleneck-sensitive expressions
```

## Driver

Compute demand may continue rising as AI workloads move from training-heavy adoption toward broader inference, enterprise deployment, and embedded software workflows.

## Mechanism

Large-scale data center expansion requires power availability, grid connection, backup generation, cooling, electrical gear, land, permits, and capital.

## Economic effect

Potential effects include increased utility capex, longer equipment backlogs, higher demand for power management and thermal infrastructure, and possible local/regional power scarcity.

## Value-capture question

The key research problem is not whether the theme is popular. The key problem is identifying who captures durable economics versus who merely absorbs higher capex, rising power costs, or valuation hype.

## Disconfirming signals to define

- Data center power demand estimates revised materially lower.
- Efficiency gains offset gross compute growth faster than expected.
- Grid/equipment bottlenecks prevent revenue conversion for supposed beneficiaries.
- Public-market expressions already discount aggressive growth assumptions.
- Regulatory or local opposition meaningfully slows expansion.
