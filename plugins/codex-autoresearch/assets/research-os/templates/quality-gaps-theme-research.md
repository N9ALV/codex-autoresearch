# Theme Research Quality Gaps

- [ ] Theme definition is explicit and falsifiable.
- [ ] Causal chain names driver, mechanism, economic effect, value-capture layer, and investable expression.
- [ ] Bear/base/bull/disconfirming or hype-unwind scenarios are documented and weights sum to 100%.
- [ ] Theme validity and theme investability are scored separately.
- [ ] Beneficiaries and victims are mapped by value-chain layer.
- [ ] Material claims have dated evidence entries or are marked as assumptions.
- [ ] Disconfirming evidence is named before the packet is marked keep/expand.
- [ ] Next research action is specific enough for another agent to execute from Git files.
